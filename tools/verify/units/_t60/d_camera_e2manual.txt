# the centre height / radius / latitude / fovy (limited_range_addition 024F7DF4: clamped, the blocked follow rate at a
# limit), turns the yaw by ax x yawStep and the view chases the targets with fixed ratios; at N steps the linear parts

# plus the frame's add still to come); the s16 yaw / latitude targets carry their truncated remainder inside the frame.
# This spec steers the stick (x, y over the whole range: the adds and the blocked limits run) with the start block off

# at its target) each step turns by its own chased value, a ramp the steps integrate (the speed-ramp position-gap rule class): the N steps
# turn by 0.92 x yawStep x c x (1 - dt) / 2 degrees less (c the chase step, at most 0.18; yawStep at most 8, the manual
# styles' param 24): 0.33 deg = 60 units at N 2, 0.50 deg = 90 units at N 4, + 1 unit truncation: abs 92 (both N).
# The latitude target: abs 1 (the carry leaves the frame end within one truncation). Not checked: the view radius and
# angles (the view chases targets the stick moves inside the frame: the speed-ramp position-gap moving-target lag; the angles are cSAngle
