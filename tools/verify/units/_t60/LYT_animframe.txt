# true60 N-step spec: nw::lyt::Animator::UpdateFrame on a general step length (frame += speed*dt, the animation crossing-check rule and 7).
# The dt-1 check rows are LYT_animframe.txt's. Domain: the frame inside [0, count) (animations start inside their range:
# setFrame / play), speeds -4..4 (the UI's play rates are 1, 0.5, 2 and their negatives), play types Once, Loop,
# Mirror and an unknown one (no bound handling). The frame count is 8 so that most inputs cross an end. Not equivalent
# by design: a frame that starts outside [0, count) and re-enters within one 30 Hz frame (Loop / Mirror on the first
# step, where the one step lands inside); the domain excludes it.
arg! 02872DFC r3 0x50000000 0x50000000
field 02872DFC r3+0xC f32 0 7.99
field 02872DFC r3+0x24 f32 -4 4
field 02872DFC r3+0x28 u32 0,1,2,5
field 02872DFC r3+0x2C u32 0,1,2,4,7,8,0xFFFFFFFF
ret! 028713B8 8
t60conv 02872DFC
# frame to float rounding; speed exact (0 after an end, the sign after a mirror); the end / wrap latch: OR over the N updates
t60tol 02872DFC r3+0xC f32 absrel 0.0001 0.00001
